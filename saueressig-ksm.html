Saueressig KSM

An end-to-end order management system managing the full workflow from intake to production, enabling cross-department collaboration across Repro, Quality, and Production.

Project Overview

KSM is an order management system developed for Saueressig, covering the entire lifecycle of an order from intake through to final production. The system connects multiple departments — Repro, Quality, and Production — into a single unified workflow, replacing disconnected manual processes with a traceable, role-based system.

Built with .NET Core and Blazor on the frontend, backed by MSSQL. The application supports role-based workflows, allowing each department to interact with orders relevant to their stage of the process while maintaining full visibility and process tracking across the organization.
